## 2.Chapter Roadmap

### First Leap (Span and Linear independence)

- A list of vectors can span a finite dimensional vector space by  taking **linear combinations**. This section defines **linear independence** , then proves [[Direct characterization of linear dependence]] that is interested.

### Second Leap (Bases)

- Now the section seeks a shortest or **linearly independent** list of vectors **spanning $V$**. The way to obtain such a list from a given vector list is by discarding linearly dependent vectors step by step. This leads to the conclusion that every finite-dimensional vector space $V$ has a basis and we can find it.

### Third Leap (Dimension  $dim(V)$)

- By the previous two sections, we know $V$ 's arbitry basis always has the same **number of vectors/bases**. The section defines the number is dimension.

## 3. Summary

> Find a tool for **classifying** vector space. Dimension is a criterion for classifying vector spaces: it separates them into infinite-dimensional spaces and finite-dimensional spaces, and finite-dimensional spaces are further classified by their specific dimension.
